Built on Apple's Screen Time

ScreenGuard uses Apple's Screen Time framework. Apple only gives the app anonymous tokens for the apps you choose; the developer cannot see which apps you chose or how you use them.

No ScreenGuard server

No account, no tracking, no ads, no ScreenGuard server. Your data stays on your devices. Insights are worked out on your device, from your own history.

iCloud sync is your choice

Off by default. If you turn it on, your locks and days off are kept in your own iCloud so they appear on your other devices. Your history, Focus Score and streaks are not synced.

Good questions.

Why does ScreenGuard need Screen Time access?

Blocking apps on iPhone is only possible through Apple's Screen Time framework. When you allow access, ScreenGuard can shield the apps you choose. See the privacy policy.

What is a lock?

A lock is a group of apps and when they're locked. There are three kinds: for a set time (now, or automatically at set times), all the time (with short Breaks when you need one, and optional free times like lunch), and a daily time limit (one shared amount of time a day for the group). Every lock also has a strength: Gentle, Firm or Strict.

What is a Gentle lock?

A Gentle lock doesn't block the app. Gardi asks you to take a breath and say what you came for, then lets you in for a few minutes. Turning back counts as a win. Firm locks keep the apps locked, with Breaks when you need one; Strict locks (Pro) can't be ended early.

What is a Break?

A Break opens the apps of an always-on lock for a few minutes, after a short breathing pause, and locks them again automatically. You choose how many Breaks you get a day.

What does Strict mode do?

Strict mode (Pro) is for locks with an end time, up to 24 hours. A strict lock can't be ended early, paused, weakened or interrupted by a Break in the app until it ends by itself.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Settings › your name › Subscriptions › ScreenGuard Pro › Cancel Subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.
